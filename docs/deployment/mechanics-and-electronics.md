# Mechanics & Electronics

Understand how mechanical design, sensing, actuation, and power constrain robot behavior.

## Key concepts

- Loads, joints, tolerances, and mechanical compliance.
- Voltage, current, resistance, signal conditioning, and grounding.
- Motors, encoders, cameras, and inertial sensors.
- Embedded interfaces and power distribution.

## Try it

Document a robot's signal and power paths. Identify sensor inputs, actuator outputs, operating limits, and calibration steps using its hardware documentation.
