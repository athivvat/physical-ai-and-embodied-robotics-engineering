# Generative Models & Foundation Models

## Overview

Generative methods used in image/video modeling and modern robot-policy architectures.

This topic guide identifies concepts to explore and an experiment to try. Use the linked documentation for implementation details.

## Prerequisites

[Transformers & Attention Architectures](transformers.md)

## Key concepts

- Autoencoders/VAE.
- latent representations.
- diffusion and denoising.
- flow matching.
- pretraining/fine-tuning.
- multimodal representation learning.
- evaluation, failure modes, and generalization.

## Try it

Train a small generative model; compare diffusion and flow-based approaches conceptually; identify their role in action generation and world modeling.

Record your setup, inputs, measurements, and failure cases. Keep the experiment small enough to repeat, and compare the result against a simple baseline.
