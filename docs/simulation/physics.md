# Physics Simulation & Robot Modeling

## Overview

Model contacts, forces, and actuation with awareness of simulator limitations.

This topic guide identifies concepts to explore and an experiment to try. Use the linked documentation for implementation details.

## Prerequisites

[Robot Kinematics & Dynamics](kinematics-and-dynamics.md)

## Key concepts

- Rigid bodies, gravity and friction.
- contact/collision models.
- articulations and joint drives.
- step size and numerical stability.
- PhysX/MuJoCo concepts.
- calibration and validation.

## Try it

Configure a stable articulated robot simulation; investigate changes in friction or contact parameters; quantify mismatch with observed hardware behavior.

Record your setup, inputs, measurements, and failure cases. Keep the experiment small enough to repeat, and compare the result against a simple baseline.
