# Robot Kinematics & Dynamics

## Overview

Motion and physical interactions of articulated robots.

This topic guide identifies concepts to explore and an experiment to try. Use the linked documentation for implementation details.

## Prerequisites

[Mathematics for Physical AI](../foundations/mathematics.md)

## Key concepts

- Coordinate transforms.
- forward/inverse kinematics.
- Jacobians.
- velocities and torques.
- rigid-body dynamics and inertia.
- joint limits.
- trajectories and collisions.

## Try it

Compute end-effector poses; implement elementary inverse kinematics; explain effects of limits and dynamics on feasible motion.

Record your setup, inputs, measurements, and failure cases. Keep the experiment small enough to repeat, and compare the result against a simple baseline.
