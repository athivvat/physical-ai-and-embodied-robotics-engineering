# Robot Policy Training with Isaac Lab

## Overview

Scale policy experiments in simulation and prepare for deployment-oriented evaluation.

This topic guide identifies concepts to explore and an experiment to try. Use the linked documentation for implementation details.

## Prerequisites

[Isaac Sim & Digital Twin Engineering](../simulation/isaac-sim.md), [Reinforcement Learning for Robotics](reinforcement-learning.md)

## Key concepts

- Isaac Lab task configuration.
- observations/actions/rewards/terminations.
- parallel environments.
- RL and imitation workflows.
- curriculum learning and domain randomization.
- policy distillation.
- checkpoints and diagnostics.

## Try it

Train a simulated manipulation policy; compare controlled experiments; evaluate robustness across randomized environments.

Record your setup, inputs, measurements, and failure cases. Keep the experiment small enough to repeat, and compare the result against a simple baseline.
